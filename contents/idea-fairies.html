Meet the Idea Fairies

You bring the magic. I bring the music.

Every great song starts with a spark, and that spark comes from you. Here, you are the Idea Fairy: the one who sprinkles in the memories, the names, the inside jokes, and the feelings that make your story yours. You don’t need to know anything about music. You’re the Muse, and your ideas are the heart of the song.

My job is the creative process. I take the ideas you share and shape them into a customized, beautiful song made just for you and your family.

How the magic works

1. You sprinkle in the ideas

Share whatever you have: a few words, a name, a favorite memory, a mood, or a full set of lyrics. Big or small, every idea counts. You can type it, upload it, or simply record yourself talking about it.

2. I handle the creative process

I take your ideas and do the work of turning them into music: writing and shaping the lyrics, choosing the style, and building the arrangement so the song sounds and feels the way you imagined.

3. A song for you and your family

Whether it’s a quinceañera, a wedding, a graduation, or a tribute to someone you love, the finished song is made to fit your family and your moment, a keepsake you can share for years to come.

4. You choose your favorite

I’ll give you a few options to listen to, so the final version feels exactly right. Best of all, it’s completely free to try.
